# The agentic map: revising the metrics

This note records what the course asks of metrics, which of the metrics written down so far survive the agentic map, and what has to change. It was researched on 2026-11-14 from the course lectures and guidelines, the Wiki, the evaluation code under `main-server/eval/`, and the design document (`agentic-map-design.md`). Nothing has been applied to the Wiki or the acceptance-test sheet. The Wiki is edited after the build; section 10 says what cannot wait that long.

## 1. In short

- Each measure says what it is checked against. Checked against the engine's own walking table and its own best answer, "everyone arrives on time" and "time together" would pass by construction. They are checked by a checker and a reference solver written apart from the engine, and the walking table's own error is reported beside them (NFR-15: 16 of 30 timed walks within tolerance).
- The latency targets become p95 with a named workload, device, and script, in place of medians.
- The privacy measure becomes a gate: no answer, card, map payload, invitation result, or socket event may reveal more about a Friend than the asker's map shows.
- The chat evaluation suites of Iterations 2 and 3 stay as regression for the flows that remain. The free-time chat suite stays among them while the old flow still answers apps that do not draw answers on the map.
- The course adds measures the design did not have: SUS and one usefulness question after each acceptance session, task success with an interval, crashes counted with exposure, key events verified in the alpha, and a lab study in place of an A/B test.
- The evaluation set, the baselines, and the thresholds are committed together before anything is run on the set. This freeze is an Iteration 5 task, done by 11/25.
- The measures as adopted are in section 5 of the design document.
